Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions about how Story Wave protects your canon? Here are the answers.

How does Story Wave detect contradictions?

Story Wave continuously extracts structured facts (ages, locations, travel times, abilities, and world rules) into a persistent database. When new chapters or scenes are written, our verification engine compares new claims against established lore and flags inconsistencies with exact line references.

Can I work on multiple books or alternate universes?

Yes! With our Multiverse Branching feature, you can fork any story universe into an independent timeline. Changes made in an alternate branch won't overwrite your primary canon, making it ideal for spinoffs and multiversal sagas.

Does Story Wave require an internet connection?

The web studio runs locally in your browser with full offline chapter editing, local storage backups, graph visualization, and browser-native voice synthesis. Cloud AI features use the Google Gemini backend API when configured.

Is my manuscript private and secure?

Yes. Your stories, characters, and bibles are stored locally on your machine. Your unpublished work is never used to train public language models.
